## About

I'm an applied machine learning practitioner and software developer working at the intersection of AI, software engineering, and real-world operations.

I currently work as an **Analyst (Analytics & Machine Learning) at Singapore LNG (SLNG)**, where I develop data-driven models, analytics applications, and decision-support tools for energy operations. My work extends beyond individual models to the systems around them, including data pipelines, backend services, web applications, APIs, software architecture, and integration with operational workflows. I enjoy connecting these different layers to turn technical ideas into useful, reliable tools.

Previously, I worked as a **Research Assistant and Software Developer at the University of Alberta**, exploring scientific machine learning for subsurface energy engineering, particularly neural surrogate models for CO₂ storage simulations. My research also extended into deep reinforcement learning, LLM-based agent workflows, and remote-sensing applications.

My interests have gradually expanded from developing and evaluating models to designing complete intelligent systems. I am especially interested in problems that combine mathematical reasoning, machine learning, optimization, and practical software engineering.

Outside my professional work, I enjoy experimenting with reinforcement learning, interactive applications, visualization, and small independent projects. I like building things that are not only technically interesting, but also tangible, testable, and fun to use.
